# Instructor guide — comparing CUT&RUN peak calls

The intervals in this practical are synthetic. Base-pair Jaccard measures the intersection divided by the union of covered genomic positions, not specificity, sensitivity, FDR or true enhancer assignment.

Facilitator prompts: What happens to overlap if one caller expands each interval? How do broad histone domains contrast with sharp transcription factor peaks? What differences arise from biological replicates, read depths and antibody controls? Refer learners to Nooranikhojasteh, Tavallaee and Orouji (Bioinformatics 2025, doi:10.1093/bioinformatics/btaf375).

Never infer one algorithm is universally best from toy overlap. Discuss orthogonal validation, reproducibility and target-specific context.
