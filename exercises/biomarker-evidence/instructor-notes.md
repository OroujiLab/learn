# Instructor guide — fictional biomarker seminar

All eight cases are invented. Months of follow-up are not a complete survival dataset suitable for estimating treatment effects, and tiny samples cannot establish a clinically valid assay.

Key issues: selection bias in establishing organoids; assay reproducibility; pre-specified thresholds; data leakage; censoring; treatment-by-biomarker interactions; distinction between prognostic and predictive biomarkers; clinical utility relative to standard care.

Ask learners to identify which variables are missing before requesting any statistical model. Never use this exercise to recommend patient treatment.
